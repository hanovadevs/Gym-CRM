### Product introduction

Running a gym involves more than keeping a member list. Membership dates affect access. Payments change balances. Renewals need attention. Trainers and classes need schedules that work together.

Mojym connects these everyday tasks. Teams can register a member, agree a monthly fee, issue an invoice, record a payment, provide a receipt and manage attendance from the same system. Owners can review current activity without piecing together separate spreadsheets and message threads.

### Member management that keeps the history together

Keep contact details, membership periods, notes, invoices, payment records and visits connected to each member. Search the directory, review a profile and archive inactive records while retaining their history.

### Memberships and monthly fees with clear dates

Create membership plans, enroll members, renew terms and manage freezes or cancellations. Owners can set an individual monthly fee and starting date during registration, then track upcoming charges and unpaid balances. In-app reminders and owner email summaries help bring approaching fee dates to attention.

### A clearer record of what is owed and what was paid

Issue invoices, record partial or full payments and review remaining balances. Download invoices and paid receipts as PDFs for sharing. Authorized corrections retain their history, so refunds, credits and reversals remain traceable.

### Attendance connected to membership access

Manage check-in and checkout with member lookup and QR-supported workflows. Access decisions consider the member's membership and configured gym policies. Authorized staff can review a denial and record a reasoned override where their permissions allow it.

### Coaching and classes without competing schedules

Maintain trainer profiles, availability and member assignments. Schedule personal-training sessions and classes with trainer, room and member conflict checks. Class bookings enforce capacity and retain cancellation history.

### Employee and salary records in the same workspace

Keep employee details, agreed monthly salary rates and salary dues organized. Record partial or full salary payments and review the remaining amount. Salary payments connect to the expense ledger, with retained correction history.

### An overview built from the gym's records

See active members, check-ins, collections, overdue invoices, recent registrations, upcoming renewals, fee reminders and scheduled classes. Branch scope and permissions determine what each user can see.

### Practical on phones, tablets and desktop

Mojym uses a restrained Silver & Midnight interface with clear sections, compact forms and readable records. Phones use a fixed bottom navigation and a More menu; desktop retains the collapsible sidebar. Tables adapt into labeled record cards, and drawers keep their close controls accessible while scrolling.

## Suggested frequently asked questions

**Who is Mojym for?**

Mojym is built for gym owners and the teams who manage members, reception, fees, attendance and coaching. Access is organized by staff role and branch scope.

**Can we set different monthly fees for different members?**

Yes. An owner can agree an individual monthly fee and starting date when registering a member, then track the member's billing dates and balances.

**Can invoices and receipts be shared?**

Invoices and paid receipts can be downloaded as PDFs, allowing the gym administrator to share them with the member.

**Does Mojym support trainers and classes?**

Yes. Implemented workflows cover trainer profiles and availability, member assignments, personal-training sessions, class schedules, capacity and bookings.

**Can staff use it on a phone?**

Yes. The web interface adapts to phones and tablets with compact layouts, labeled record cards and mobile navigation.

**Does recording a payment charge a member automatically?**

No. The current product records payments received by the gym. It does not process online payments or automatically debit member accounts.

Use the FAQ questions that fit the existing page format; do not add a large FAQ block solely to fill space.

## Technical context for the website agent

Mojym is a modular web application using Next.js, React and TypeScript, with PostgreSQL, Drizzle migrations and application-managed Better Auth authentication. The deployed application uses Vercel and Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL. Supabase is the database host; the product does not depend on Supabase Auth or direct browser access to database tables.

Business access is checked on the server using organization membership, role and branch scope. Financial workflows use database constraints and transactional services, with retained audit and correction histories. Present these as implementation choices, not as a security certification or a guarantee that defects are impossible.

The final UI pass was checked on the canonical application across 17 implemented gym/admin screens at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels, including drawer reachability, document overflow and client errors. This is useful internal quality evidence; public copy does not need test counts, migration numbers or deployment hashes.
